Everything a daily player needs, nothing it doesn't.

The window stays calm. The work happens underneath.

Synced lyrics

Lyrics scroll line by line with the song and are found for you. Click any line to jump to it.

An equalizer you can trust

Ten bands, a preamp, a Tame bass preset and a limiter that stops clipping. Switch between stereo and mono.

Crossfade that behaves

Songs blend when they end on their own. When you skip, the next one starts instantly.

Your files and YouTube Music

Drop in your library or search online. Missing covers are found once and kept.

A widget for your desktop

A square cover that floats above your windows. Hover for controls, drag a corner to resize.

Discord and Spotify

Sign in with Discord, show what you're listening to, and bring over Spotify playlists with a link or a CSV.
